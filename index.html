Last Updated & Effective Date: October 14, 2026

Welcome to Ai step Counter ("we", "our", or "the App"). We are firmly committed to protecting your privacy, maintaining data transparency, and ensuring that you retain complete authority over your physical activity and fitness telemetry. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, process, store, and protect your information when you use the Ai step Counter mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

To provide accurate step tracking, calorie calculation, and personalized fitness coaching, we collect the following types of information:

A. Physical Activity & Hardware Sensor Telemetry
  • Step Sensor Data: We access device sensors (Sensor.TYPE_STEP_COUNTER and Sensor.TYPE_STEP_DETECTOR) and Android Activity Recognition to detect walking, running, and daily steps.
  • Workout Metrics: Duration, estimated active minutes, calories burned, average pace, cadence, and user-logged exercise routines (gym sets/reps, sleep duration, sports modes).
B. Location Data (GPS Route Tracking)
  • Precise / Approximate Location: Collected only while you are actively recording an Outdoor Walk, Outdoor Run, or Cycling workout session.
  • Purpose: Used exclusively on-device to map your workout path and calculate real distance and elevation changes.
  • Notice: We do not track location continuously in the background when no workout is active, nor do we sell location coordinates to third-party brokers.
C. User Profile & Biometric Attributes
  • Biometric Baselines: Age, Gender, Height, and Weight provided during onboarding. These are required strictly for stride length calibration and Basal Metabolic Rate (BMR) calorie expenditure estimation.
  • Account Data: Display name and email address (when using Google Sign-In or Email authentication).

2. How We Use Your Information

We process collected data solely to deliver and refine your fitness experience:

  • To compute real-time daily step counts, progress rings, and weekly wave trend charts.
  • To power Coach Shahdab (your in-app fitness mentor) with contextual guidance and milestone celebrations.
  • To administer streaks, streak freezes, and unlocked hexagonal achievement awards.
  • To manage optional friends leaderboards and shared 7-day walking challenges.
  • To verify AI Step Pro subscription status and remove advertisements.

3. Advertising & Subscription Policies

  • Free Plan Advertising: The free tier of Ai step Counter is supported by Google AdMob banners and optional rewarded video ads (used to earn complimentary Streak Freezes).
  • 24-Hour New User Delay: Newly onboarded users enjoy a 24-hour ad-free welcome experience. Banner ads activate only after 24 hours of app installation.
  • Zero In-Workout Disruptions: We strictly prohibit intrusive interstitial or full-screen advertisements during active running or walking workouts.
  • AI Step Pro: Upgrading to AI Step Pro (₹299/month promotional plan) immediately and permanently removes all advertisements across the application.

4. Data Storage, Security & Retention

We implement industry-standard encryption protocols (TLS/HTTPS in transit and AES-256 for cloud backups). You have the right to request deletion of your account at any time.

All activity logs can be exported directly from the app in structured JSON format under Profile → Export Personal Activity Data.

5. User Rights & Data Deletion

You maintain complete control over your information:

  • Access & Portability: View and export your step history and workout sessions at any time.
  • Account Deletion: You can permanently purge your account, biometric metrics, streaks, and fitness history with one click via Profile → Delete Account & Data.

6. Permissions Requested

  • android.permission.ACTIVITY_RECOGNITION: Detects steps and active exercise.
  • android.permission.ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION: Maps outdoor running/walking tracks.
  • android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Delivers daily goal reminders and streak alerts.
  • android.permission.INTERNET: Syncs cloud achievements, leaderboards, and billing verification.

7. Children's Privacy

Ai step Counter is not directed toward children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children.
